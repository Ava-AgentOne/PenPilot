# Projects and shapes test (build step 6, second half)

About 15 minutes on the Mac, plus one short plot. You need: the plotter, an A4 sheet and
a pen. Install PenPilot 0.7.0 first (quit the old one, drag the new one into Applications,
and choose **Replace**).

## 1. Shapes

1. Under **drawings**, press **Border**. A double line appears just inside the page margins.
2. Press **Rectangle**. Drag its corner dot: it stretches freely (it doesn't keep its shape).
   In its settings, set **corner radius** to 5 for rounded corners.
3. Press **Circle**. Change its **width** only: it becomes an oval.
4. Select the rectangle, set **lines** to 1, and under **fill** choose **Hatch: lines**.
   It fills solid.
5. Add some text inside the border too (e.g. your name), for a small certificate or card.

## 2. Save, close and open again

1. Press **Save** (or ⌘S). Choose where to save it and give it a name, e.g. `Card`.
   The name shows under the buttons.
2. Move something a little. It now says **changes not saved**.
3. Quit PenPilot (⌘Q). It asks before closing: choose **Close without saving**.
4. Open PenPilot again and press **Open…** (or ⌘O). Choose `Card.penpilot`. The page comes
   back exactly as you saved it: the shapes, fill, text, pens and paper.
5. Optional: add a photo, save, and open it again. It comes back with the same lines,
   not redrawn.

## 3. Export SVG

1. Press **Export SVG…** and save `Card.svg`.
2. Double-click it (it opens in Safari or your browser): it shows the page's lines, hatching
   included, in each pen's colour.

## 4. Plot it

Plot the card on A4 (Frame first if you like). It should match the screen.

## Report back

- Do the shapes look right on paper (corners, double border, the filled rectangle)?
- Did **Open** bring everything back as it was?
- Did the **Export SVG** file look right?
- Any of this awkward to use?
