# Workspace toolbar, undo and zoom (1.0.0)

About 10 minutes on the Mac, no plotting needed. Install PenPilot 1.0.0 first (quit the old
one, drag the new one into Applications, and choose **Replace**).

At the top of the page there's now a small toolbar: Undo, Redo | Save | Duplicate, Remove |
zoom out, zoom %, zoom in, Fit | pen-up moves.

1. **Undo / Redo:** add a rectangle, move it, resize it, change its pen. Press **Undo** (or
   ⌘Z) a few times: each change goes back, one at a time. **Redo** (⇧⌘Z) brings them back.
   Remove a drawing, then Undo: it comes back.
2. **One step for quick changes:** select a drawing and press → five times quickly, or drag a
   photo/fill slider. One Undo puts it all back.
3. **Typing:** in a text's box, type something, then ⌘Z: it undoes the typing, not the page.
4. **Duplicate:** select a drawing, press **Duplicate** (or ⌘D). A copy appears 5 mm down and
   right, selected, ready to move.
5. **Zoom:** press **+** a few times, or pinch on the trackpad over a part of the page: it zooms
   towards that spot. Scroll with two fingers to move around. Drag a drawing and its corner
   dot while zoomed in. Click the **%** (or ⌘0) to fit the page again.
6. **Pen-up moves:** the last button hides the thin grey lines of the pen's moves in the air;
   press again to show them. PenPilot remembers your choice.
7. **Save** from the toolbar (or ⌘S) still saves the project.
