# Hatch fill test (build step 5)

About 15 minutes on the Mac, plus plotting time. You need: the plotter, an A4 sheet, a
fine pen (0.3–0.5 mm), and `ghfh.svg` (the traced image on your Desktop) or any SVG of
filled shapes. Install PenPilot 0.5.0 first (quit the old one, drag the new one into
Applications, and choose **Replace**).

## 1. Fill a traced drawing

1. **Add drawing or photo…** → `ghfh.svg`. A notice says it's made of filled
   shapes. Press **Fill them with hatching**.
2. A **fill** box appears under the drawing's settings, e.g. "fill (10 closed shapes)".
   The shapes now show diagonal lines inside them.
3. Try the choices in the box:
   - **Hatch: lines** — parallel lines.
   - **Hatch: cross-hatch** — lines both ways (darker, takes about twice as long).
   - **Concentric** — shrinking copies of the outline, like contour lines.
   - **No fill** — back to outlines only.
4. **Line spacing**: for a solid look, set it to about your pen's line width (0.4 mm for
   a 0.4 mm pen). Bigger spacing gives a lighter, "shaded" look and plots faster.
5. **Angle** turns the lines. **Draw the outlines too** adds a clean edge round each shape.
6. **Frame**, then **Plot** (lines, about 0.5 mm spacing).

## 2. Filled letters

1. **Add text**, pick an Arabic or handwriting **traced** font (e.g. Noto Naskh or
   Caveat), and type something short: `عيد ميلاد سعيد` or `Hello`. Letter size 20–30 mm.
2. Press **Filled** (next to One line). Each letter is traced round its edge and filled
   with hatching, so the text looks like the font on screen. Letter dots are filled too.
3. Plot it with spacing about your pen width.

## Report back

- Do the filled shapes and letters look **solid** on paper? Which spacing looked best
  with your pen?
- Did any hatching go **outside** a shape, or into a hole (inside an "o", the eyes)?
- Is the plot time shown close to the real time?
- Which fill style do you like best?
